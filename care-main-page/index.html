Refocus Care

Ongoing support for modern minds

Refocus Care is our continuity-of-care service, designed to support individuals after diagnosis, between life stages, and through periods of neurological and hormonal change. This is where personalised treatment begins and support continues.

What Refocus Care covers

Structured clinical pathways, not one-off appointments

All services are clinician-led, evidence-based, and aligned with NICE guidance while remaining responsive to emerging neuroscience and real-world patient needs.

Specialist assessments exploring how hormonal transitions, including early perimenopause and menopause, interact with brain function, emotional regulation, cognition, and ADHD traits. Our philosophy

Brains don’t exist in isolation

They exist within bodies, relationships, careers, and changing hormonal landscapes.

That’s why Refocus does not separate psychiatry, neuroscience, and hormone health into silos.

We prioritise thoughtful medicine, shared decision-making, and care that adapts as lives evolve. We bring together psychiatry, advanced nursing practice, neuroscience, and hormone-brain expertise to deliver thoughtful, ethical, and long-term clinical care.

Refocus Care is our ongoing clinical pathway for individuals who require structured, medically led follow-up after assessment.

It includes:

  • Women’s Neurohormone Assessments
  • ADHD / Neurodevelopmental Medication Pathway (pending CQC registration)
  • Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) pathway

Refocus Care is designed for:

  • Women experiencing cognitive, emotional or attentional changes linked to hormonal transition
  • Individuals diagnosed with ADHD who require medication initiation or ongoing monitoring
  • Patients seeking integrated psychiatric and hormonal support

Our approach recognises that brain function, hormones and mental health are interconnected. Rather than treating symptoms in isolation, we take a whole-brain, whole-person perspective.

Every care plan is personalised, structured and consultant-led.

Our Women’s Neurohormone pathway is a specialist consultation service exploring the interaction between:

  • ADHD and female presentation
  • Perimenopause and menopause
  • Mood changes and emotional regulation
  • Brain fog and cognitive shifts
  • Sleep disruption and burnout

The assessment includes:

  • Detailed clinical and hormonal history
  • Review of previous treatments
  • Risk assessment and medical screening
  • Individualised formulation

Where appropriate, treatment options may include:

  • ADHD medication optimisation
  • HRT discussion and prescribing (pending CQC registration)
  • Lifestyle and sleep regulation strategies
  • Cognitive and brain-health recommendations

HRT prescribing follows recognised UK clinical guidance and includes careful risk screening and monitoring.

Our focus is safety, clarity and evidence-informed decision-making, not trend-driven treatment.

For individuals diagnosed with ADHD, we offer a structured medication pathway (pending CQC approval).

This includes:

  • Medication discussion and informed consent
  • Careful initiation and titration
  • Regular monitoring appointments
  • Side-effect and response tracking
  • Communication with your GP regarding shared care where appropriate

We follow national prescribing standards and ensure:

  • Physical health screening prior to initiation
  • Ongoing safety monitoring
  • Clear documentation
  • Transparent pricing and review structure

Shared care agreements depend on local GP policies, and we will guide you through this process.

Our aim is stability, safety and sustainable improvement, not rushed prescribing.
