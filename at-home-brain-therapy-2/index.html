Brain health should move beyond symptom-management — and become more proactive, personalised, and neuroscience-informed.

Our at-home photobiomodulation pathways bring clinically informed brain support into everyday life through non-invasive near-infrared light therapy — designed to support brain energy, cognitive performance, and long-term brain wellness.

Whether you are struggling with burnout, brain fog, poor focus, cognitive fatigue, or simply looking to better support your brain as you age, PBM offers a modern, drug-free approach to brain optimisation and neurological wellbeing.

The science

Backed by emerging neuroscience.

Photobiomodulation is an evolving area of neuroscience research, with thousands of published studies exploring how near-infrared light may support cellular energy production, neuroplasticity, blood flow, and cognitive function.

PBM works primarily through the mitochondria — the energy-producing structures inside cells — helping improve the production of ATP, the fuel the brain relies on to function efficiently.

Research is also exploring the potential role of PBM in supporting cerebral oxygenation, reducing oxidative stress and inflammation, improving neuronal resilience, and supporting the brain’s natural recovery and waste-clearance systems.

Better brain energy may support better brain function.

Studies have explored potential benefits in:

  • Mental clarity and focus
  • Brain fog and fatigue
  • Cognitive resilience and healthy ageing
  • Memory and processing
  • Mood and emotional wellbeing
