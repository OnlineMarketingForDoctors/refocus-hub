At Refocus Diagnostics, every assessment is comprehensive, consultant-led and grounded in NICE-aligned clinical practice.

We offer specialist pathways for:

  • Children & Young People - ADHD and Autism Assessments
  • General Child & Adolescent Psychiatry Assessments
  • Women’s ADHD & Neurohormone Assessments

Each assessment includes:

  • A detailed developmental and clinical history
  • Structured diagnostic interviews
  • Standardised, evidence-based assessment tools
  • Multi-informant questionnaires (parents, school, self-report where appropriate)
  • Careful review of functional impact across education, work, relationships and wellbeing
  • Screening for co-existing conditions such as anxiety, mood difficulties, trauma, sleep disruption and hormonal factors

For women, we also explore hormonal transitions (including perimenopause and menopause) and how these may interact with attention, mood and cognitive function.

Following your assessment, you will receive:

  • A comprehensive diagnostic report
  • A clear clinical formulation
  • Individualised recommendations
  • A structured feedback session to explain findings and next steps

We focus not only on diagnosis, but on understanding the whole person.

Assessments at Refocus are conducted by GMC-registered Consultant Psychiatrists and experienced clinicians working within national clinical guidelines and UK best-practice standards.

Our reports are suitable to support:

  • School and SENCO discussions
  • Exam access arrangements
  • University disability services
  • Access to Work applications
  • Communication with your GP

Most NHS services accept appropriately completed private consultant assessments. However, shared care arrangements for medication vary between GP practices and local NHS trusts.

We provide:

  • Clear, structured documentation
  • Guidance on shared care discussions
  • Professional communication where required

Our aim is to support continuity of care and integration with existing services wherever possible.

Following your assessment, we provide a clear roadmap tailored to you or your child.

Depending on clinical need, this may include:

ADHD Care Pathway

  • Medication discussion and initiation (if appropriate)
  • Structured titration and monitoring
  • School or workplace support guidance
  • Psychological or coaching referrals

Women’s ADHD & Neurohormone Pathway

  • Integrated review of hormonal and attentional symptoms
  • Medication options where indicated
  • Cognitive, sleep and lifestyle strategies
  • Ongoing specialist review

Child & Adolescent Psychiatry

  • Diagnostic clarification and formulation
  • Emotional and behavioural support planning
  • Medication where clinically appropriate
  • Therapeutic recommendations

Refocus is designed as a connected hub: Diagnostics → Care → Neuroscience

Some families or adults choose to continue with ongoing psychiatric care. Others may explore optional neuroscience services such as QEEG brain mapping, neurofeedback, Exomind TMS or transcranial photobiomodulation.

There is no obligation to continue beyond your assessment. You may take your report and pursue support elsewhere if you wish.

Our role is to provide clarity, expertise and informed choice, so you can move forward with confidence.
