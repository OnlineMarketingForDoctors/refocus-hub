Women’s ADHD & perimenopause

ADHD assessments for women approaching perimenopause

A holistic neuropsychiatric pathway designed for women at midlife. We recognise that ADHD, hormones, and mental health intersect, so you receive clarity rather than fragmented explanations.

Why it’s missed

Why ADHD is often missed in women

Many women with ADHD are not identified in childhood. The signs can be subtle and easily attributed to personality traits or life circumstances.

  • Masking Difficulties

    Girls often mask difficulties by overcompensating, presenting as anxious, perfectionistic, or emotionally sensitive rather than ‘hyperactive’.

  • Academic Performance

    Many perform well academically until demands increase, making ADHD harder to identify in childhood.

  • Hormonal Changes

    As oestrogen levels fluctuate during perimenopause, dopamine regulation becomes more vulnerable, and ADHD traits may become unmasked or significantly worse.

Recognise this?

Common symptoms during perimenopause

  • Brain fog and forgetfulness
  • Feeling overwhelmed
  • Coping strategies no longer working
  • Emotional lability
  • Concentration difficulties

These symptoms may reflect perimenopause, burnout, anxiety, sleep disturbance, or neurodevelopmental vulnerability interacting with hormonal change.

This is why our approach is holistic and menopause-informed.

Is this assessment right for you?

  • A woman approaching or experiencing perimenopause
  • Not previously diagnosed with ADHD but now struggling with focus, organisation, or overwhelm
  • Unsure whether difficulties are due to ADHD, hormonal change, or both

What’s included

What the assessment includes

A comprehensive, evidence-based assessment designed to understand your unique experience and provide clear answers.

Clinical Interview & Developmental Exploration

  • Detailed clinical interview exploring lifelong attention, organisation, and emotional regulation patterns
  • Mapping of symptoms across development, education, work, relationships, and parenting
  • Exploration of recent changes linked to hormonal transition

DIVA-5 Diagnostic Interview

  • Gold-standard structured diagnostic interview aligned with DSM-5 ADHD criteria
  • Assesses childhood and adult ADHD symptoms
  • Explores functional impairment across multiple life domains
  • Helps distinguish ADHD from anxiety, burnout, or mood disorders

MOXO AI ADHD Test

  • FDA-cleared computer-based test
  • Provides objective data on attention, impulsivity, hyperactivity, and timing
  • Tested under real-life distractors
  • Supports clinical decision-making alongside interview and history

Adult Screening Questionnaires

  • ADHD symptom and impairment scales
  • Anxiety and mood screening
  • Functional and occupational impact measures
  • Informant questionnaires from partner, family member, or colleague

Perimenopause & Menopause Screening

  • Menstrual and hormonal history
  • Vasomotor symptoms (hot flushes, night sweats)
  • Sleep disturbance assessment
  • Cognitive changes and brain fog evaluation
  • Optional blood tests and qEEG brain mapping when appropriate

MDT Discussion & Formulation

  • All cases reviewed in MDT with Medical Director
  • Holistic consideration of neurodevelopmental history
  • Integration of hormonal transition and mental health context
  • Balanced, evidence-based, and individualised recommendations

Booking information

Who this clinic is for

This specialist clinic is for women aged 40+ who are experiencing ADHD-related difficulties that have worsened during perimenopause or menopause, such as changes in focus, emotional regulation, mental energy, and cognitive clarity.

We focus on the interaction between ADHD, hormones, cognition, mood, energy, and executive functioning, for a holistic understanding and support.

Important information

  • This clinic is not suitable for acute mental health presentations.
  • It does not assess or treat bipolar disorder, psychosis, or active suicidal ideation.

If you are happy to proceed, please complete the brief pre-booking screening.
