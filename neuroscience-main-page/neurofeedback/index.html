Neurofeedback at Refocus

Train your brain. Support self-regulation. Build resilience.

Your brain is always generating electrical activity. Neurofeedback uses gentle, real-time feedback from that activity to help your brain practise regulation, the same way a mirror helps you adjust posture. Brain training

What is neurofeedback?

Neurofeedback (also called EEG biofeedback) is a form of brain training.

Small sensors sit on the scalp and read brainwave activity. Software translates those signals into feedback, often through sound or visuals, so your brain can “notice” patterns and gradually adjust.

How it works

Measure. Analyse. Feedback. Learn.

  • Non-invasive
  • No medication
  • Brain training

  1. 01

    Measure

    We measure brain activity (EEG) using sensors on the scalp.

  2. 02

    Analyse

    The system analyses patterns in real time (hundreds of readings per second).

  3. 03

    Feedback

    You receive feedback (audio/visual changes) linked to what your brain is doing in that moment.

  4. 04

    Learn

    Your brain learns through repetition, reinforcing helpful states and reducing “stuck” patterns, over time.

This is why neurofeedback is often described as training rather than “treatment”: we’re supporting the brain’s capacity to self-regulate, not forcing it to do anything.

Why NeurOptimal®

Why we use NeurOptimal® at Refocus

NeurOptimal® offers a gentle, non-invasive form of Dynamical Neurofeedback® designed to support flexibility, resilience and self-regulation.

During a session, small sensors placed on the scalp and ears monitor moment-to-moment electrical activity while you relax and listen to music. When the system detects a change in the activity it is monitoring, the music briefly pauses, providing real-time feedback that acts like a mirror for the brain.

There is no difficult task to complete, and the system does not push the brain towards a fixed target. The sensors only record activity; they do not deliver electricity or magnetic stimulation. People choose NeurOptimal® as part of their wellbeing routine to explore greater calm, focus, adaptability and resilience in everyday life. Experiences and outcomes vary, and NeurOptimal® is offered as a general wellness brain training system rather than a diagnostic test or treatment for a medical or mental health condition.

  • Real-time feedback

    NeurOptimal® continuously monitors your brain activity and reflects subtle shifts back to the central nervous system so your brain can self-adjust.

  • Non-directive and gentle

    The system does not push the brain toward specific targets or frequencies; instead, it mirrors your brain’s activity and supports its own adaptive processes.

  • Whole-system focus

    Because NeurOptimal® engages the brain as a whole, many people notice benefits across stress resilience, sleep, emotional balance, and focus.

  • No diagnosis required

    NeurOptimal® is a brain training tool, not a medical treatment, and does not diagnose or treat specific disorders.

In practice, you sit comfortably, engage with subtle feedback cues (often sound or visual), and your brain does the work, with no conscious effort needed on your part.

CBT and neurofeedback

CBT and neurofeedback: how they fit together

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) is a structured psychological therapy that helps people identify patterns of thought and behaviour that contribute to distress and learn new, more adaptive ways of thinking and acting. It’s one of the most evidence-supported therapies for anxiety, mood and stress-related conditions.

Enhancing learning readinessCalmer, more focused

Neurofeedback supports brain regulation and calm focus, which can make the cognitive work in CBT (e.g., restructuring thoughts, practising new behaviours) easier and more effective. Some research suggests that when the brain is less dysregulated, people may engage more consistently with therapeutic exercises.

Addressing physiological patternsMind and nervous system

While CBT tackles thought and behaviour patterns, neurofeedback directly engages the brain’s self-regulatory networks. Together they can offer a whole-person approach, addressing what you think and how your nervous system responds.

Adjunct outcomesBetter together

Studies have found that neurofeedback can be effective alongside CBT in reducing symptoms like anxiety and improving emotional regulation, supporting the idea that combining them can be more impactful than either alone for some clients.

At Refocus, we integrate neurofeedback seamlessly with therapeutic work (including CBT, coaching, or other supports) so your brain and behaviour both have space to shift.

What it can help with

What neurofeedback can help with

Neurofeedback is used by many people to support:

  • Focus and mental stamina

  • Emotional regulation and reactivity

  • Sleep quality and recovery

  • Stress resilience and coping

Because neurofeedback supports regulation, it can be relevant for people with neurodivergent profiles (e.g., ADHD/autistic traits), performance goals, or anyone aiming for steadier functioning.

Important note: outcomes vary by person. We do not guarantee cures or specific results.

Safety first

Is neurofeedback safe?

Neurofeedback is a non-invasive brain training approach that uses EEG sensors to help the brain learn healthier regulation patterns.

It is generally safe for children and adults of all ages.

Some individuals may need additional clinical review or tailored protocols, particularly those with:

  • A history of seizures
  • Complex trauma
  • Severe mental health conditions

At Refocus, neurofeedback is always delivered within a clinically governed framework, ensuring safety, adaptability, and evidence-informed care.
