Explained simply

iSyncWave™ brain mapping explained simply

What is iSyncWave™?

iSyncWave™ is a comfortable, non-invasive EEG brain scan that measures how your brain works — including attention, processing speed, and how different brain areas communicate.

  • No needles
  • No radiation
  • No discomfort

Why isn’t a questionnaire enough?

Questionnaires rely on memory, self-awareness and how symptoms look on the outside. But many people — especially children, women, and neurodivergent individuals — mask or compensate.

iSyncWave™ allows us to:

  • Look directly at brain activity
  • Understand why things feel hard
  • Explain strengths as well as challenges

Safety first

Is qEEG brain mapping safe?

qEEG is considered very safe and is widely used in both clinical and research settings with children, young people, and adults. The sensors only record activity — nothing is sent into the brain.

Most people experience no side effects. Occasionally, some may notice mild and temporary effects such as tiredness, a mild headache, or brief scalp sensitivity from the sensors.

qEEG may be postponed or adapted if someone is experiencing:

  • Acute mental health distress (such as severe agitation or psychosis)
  • A recent head injury or concussion
  • Scalp conditions or infections that prevent sensor placement

At Refocus, every qEEG assessment is completed within a clinically governed framework and interpreted by experienced clinicians. qEEG is often used as a gateway into our neuroscience programmes, helping to guide personalised neurofeedback, photobiomodulation, or Exomind pathways — and to support deeper understanding in complex diagnostic assessments.
