Child & adolescent autism

Understanding your child as a whole person

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a neurodevelopmental difference, not a single presentation. Children and young people experience the world, relationships, communication, and sensory input in unique ways. At Refocus, our aim is to understand your child as a whole person, their strengths, challenges, environment, and development, so families can access the right support with confidence.

All our ASD assessments are NHS-recognised and aligned with NICE guidance, delivered by an experienced multidisciplinary team (MDT).

Is it autism?

Is an autism assessment right for my child?

Autism can present very differently from one child to another and may change with age. Some children show clear early signs, while others mask their difficulties, particularly girls and cognitively able young people.

Social Communication & Interaction

  • Differences in eye contact, facial expression, or gestures
  • Difficulty starting or maintaining conversations
  • Literal interpretation of language or difficulty understanding social nuance
  • Preference for solitary play or specific friendships

Behaviour, Routines & Interests

  • Strong need for predictability or routine
  • Distress with unexpected change
  • Highly focused or intense interests
  • Repetitive behaviours or movements

Sensory Processing

  • Sensitivity to noise, light, textures, or smells
  • Seeking sensory input (movement, pressure, spinning)
  • Overwhelm in busy or unpredictable environments

Autistic traits can overlap with ADHD, anxiety, learning differences, or trauma, making specialist assessment essential.

Why it matters

Why timely and accurate diagnosis matters

Without understanding why a child struggles, they may be:

  • Mislabelled as ‘difficult’, ‘anxious’, or ‘oppositional’
  • Pushed to cope in environments that overwhelm them
  • Left without appropriate school adjustments or support

A clear diagnosis can:

  • Reduce self-blame and shame
  • Improve understanding at home and school
  • Open access to tailored educational and therapeutic support
  • Help young people develop a positive sense of identity

Our approach

Our autism assessment approach at Refocus

ASD assessment requires depth, structure, and clinical expertise. We follow NICE-compliant pathways and use gold-standard diagnostic tools, alongside comprehensive information from families and schools. Every Refocus ASD assessment includes:

  • Expert-Led Assessment

    Assessment led by highly experienced clinicians, including consultant child & adolescent psychiatrists

  • ADI-R Interview

    A detailed developmental history appointment with parents/carers using ADI-R

  • ADOS-2 Assessment

    A structured ADOS-2 assessment with the child or young person

  • Comprehensive Questionnaires

    Evidence-based questionnaires completed by parents/carers, school (teachers or SENCO), and the young person (where age-appropriate)

The gold standard

Gold-standard assessment tools we use

ADI-RAutism Diagnostic Interview - Revised

The ADI-R is a gold-standard developmental history interview conducted with parents or carers by a consultant psychiatrist.

It explores:

  • Early childhood development
  • Social communication and interaction
  • Play, imagination, routines, and behaviours
  • How traits have changed over time

This provides crucial longitudinal insight that cannot be captured by observation alone.

ADOS-2Autism Diagnostic Observation Schedule

The ADOS-2 is a structured, standardised observation carried out directly with the child or young person by a highly trained clinician.

It looks at:

  • Communication and social interaction
  • Use of language and non-verbal communication
  • Play, imagination, and flexibility

Together, ADI-R + ADOS-2 form the internationally recognised gold standard for ASD diagnosis.
