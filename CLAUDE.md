# refocus-hub

## Search indexing: this site must stay no-indexed

Until the owner explicitly says the site is ready to launch, it must not be indexed by search engines.

- Every HTML page must include `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` in its `<head>`. If a framework generates pages, set it in the shared layout or metadata config so no page is missed.
- `vercel.json` sends an `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` header on every response. Keep it, and merge any new `vercel.json` settings into the existing file rather than replacing it.
- Do not add `Disallow: /` to `robots.txt`. A blocked crawler never fetches the page, so it never sees the noindex and can still list the bare URL.
- Do not generate a sitemap or submit the site to Google Search Console.
- When the owner says to launch, remove the meta tag and the `X-Robots-Tag` header together.

## Publishing

Push every change straight to `main`.

## Homepage

`index.html`, `styles.css` and `main.js` at the repo root are the homepage. It shares its design system (colours, type, header, footer, reviews, team, FAQ) with the adult ADHD landing page in `OnlineMarketingForDoctors/refocus`. Links to other pages point to the live site at https://refocushub.co.uk/. Services that have no page on the live site stay unlinked.

## Inner pages

`prices/`, `contact/`, `learning-page/`, `neuro-blog/` and `at-home-brain-therapy-2/` keep the live site's slugs. They are generated by `tools/build_pages.py`, which takes the head, header and footer from the homepage `index.html`, so run `python3 tools/build_pages.py .` after changing the homepage header or footer, or after editing a page's content in the script. Their shared styles and scripts are `pages.css` and `pages.js`. Links between local pages are root-relative (for example `/prices/`). Blog article links that have no local page yet still point to the live site. The at-home helmets are ordered by phone ("Call to order").

The assessment pages (`diagnosis-main-page/` and its four child pages, `adult-adhd-assessment/`, `adult-autism-assessment/` and `care-main-page/adhd-assessments-for-women-approaching-perimenopause/`) are generated the same way by `tools/build_diagnosis.py`, which reuses the shell and helpers in `tools/build_pages.py` and the homepage's reviews and regulated strip. The neuroscience and care pages (`neuroscience-main-page/` and its four child pages `qeeg-brain-mapping/`, `exomind/`, `neurofeedback/`, `photobiomodulation/`, plus `care-main-page/`, `neurohormone-clinic/`, `care-main-page/adhd-medication-treatment-pathway/` and `care-main-page/hrt-treatment-pathway/`) are generated by `tools/build_neuro.py`, which reuses the blocks in `tools/build_diagnosis.py`. Their extra styles use the `nc-` prefix in `pages.css`. Blog articles keep their live slugs at the site root (for example `can-hormone-therapy-improve-neurodivergent-symptoms/`) and are generated by `tools/build_blog.py`; add an article by adding an entry to `ARTICLES`, and point its card in `POSTS` in `tools/build_pages.py` at the local slug. Their styles use the `ar-` prefix in `pages.css`.

The main menu, with its dropdowns, is defined in `tools/build_nav.py` (sections listed in its `HIDDEN` set, currently Brain care, are left out of the menu but their pages still exist), which writes it into `index.html` and `about-us/index.html`. After changing the menu, the header or the footer, run `tools/build_nav.py` and then all five page scripts (`build_pages.py`, `build_diagnosis.py`, `build_neuro.py`, `build_blog.py`, `build_consult.py`).

The free 15-minute consultation page (`free-consultation/`) is generated by `tools/build_consult.py`, and every "Book a free call" button on the site links to it. Its form is a placeholder that opens the visitor's email app with the details filled in; replace it with the real booking or CRM form when that is ready.
