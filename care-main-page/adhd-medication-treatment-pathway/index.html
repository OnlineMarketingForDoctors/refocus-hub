What the pathway will include

Safe, structured ADHD prescribing

For individuals diagnosed with ADHD, we will offer a structured medication pathway across adolescence and adulthood, with collaborative, individualised medication decisions.

  • Discussion & informed consent

    Medication discussion and informed consent, so every decision is shared.

  • Careful initiation & titration

    Starting gently and adjusting carefully to find what works for you.

  • Regular monitoring

    Regular monitoring appointments with side-effect and response tracking.

  • GP shared care

    Communication with your GP regarding shared care where appropriate.

Our standards

We follow national prescribing standards

Our aim is stability, safety and sustainable improvement, not rushed prescribing.

  • Physical health screening

    Physical health screening prior to initiation.

  • Ongoing safety monitoring

    Blood pressure, heart rate, weight and wellbeing reviewed throughout.

  • Clear documentation

    Clear records you and your GP can rely on.

  • Transparent pricing

    A transparent pricing and review structure.

Shared care agreements depend on local GP policies, and we will guide you through this process.

Refocus Care is our ongoing clinical pathway for individuals who require structured, medically led follow-up after assessment.

It includes:

  • Women’s Neurohormone Assessments
  • ADHD / Neurodevelopmental Medication Pathway (pending CQC registration)
  • Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) pathway

Refocus Care is designed for:

  • Women experiencing cognitive, emotional or attentional changes linked to hormonal transition
  • Individuals diagnosed with ADHD who require medication initiation or ongoing monitoring
  • Patients seeking integrated psychiatric and hormonal support

Our approach recognises that brain function, hormones and mental health are interconnected. Rather than treating symptoms in isolation, we take a whole-brain, whole-person perspective.

Every care plan is personalised, structured and consultant-led.
